Technical Data Collection & Processing Logic

Our architectural blueprint strictly enforces Data Minimization. We collect and process only the minimal technical data categories required to deliver authentic Islamic services:

1.1 Geographic & Spatial Telemetry (GPS / Network Coordinates)

Operational Purpose: Accurate calculation of astronomical Islamic prayer times (Fajr, Sunrise, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib, Isha) is strictly dependent upon the user's exact geographical latitude, longitude, and elevation relative to the solar zenith. Furthermore, calculating the precise spherical trigonometric vector toward the Holy Kaaba in Makkah al-Mukarramah requires device spatial coordinates.

📍
Zero-Exfiltration Guarantee: Geographic coordinates acquired via Android Location Services are evaluated entirely on-device in volatile RAM. Coordinates are never transmitted to our servers, logged to analytics backends, or shared with third parties. Once prayer schedules and Qibla bearings are computed, transient coordinates are purged.

1.2 Acoustic Input & Speech Recognition

Operational Purpose: To provide accessible, hands-free Quranic discovery, users may search for specific Ayahs or Surahs using voice input.

Processing Mechanism: Audio streams captured by the device microphone are routed through the native Android system SpeechRecognizer API. Voice bytes are analyzed ephemerally in device memory to convert speech into text search queries. Audio waveforms are never recorded, stored on persistent disk, or uploaded to external voice intelligence databases.

1.3 User Identity & Cloud Synchronization (Optional)

Guest-First Philosophy: Quran Academy does not require registration. Users can recite the full Quran, listen to audio recitations, read Hadith, and utilize prayer calculators with 100% functionality without creating an account.

Optional Cloud Sync: Should a user voluntarily authenticate using Google Sign-In, we store only their validated Google Display Name, Email Address, and an opaque Firebase User ID (UID). This identity is utilized solely as a cryptographic partition to synchronize personal reading bookmarks, Khatam progress, and favorite supplications across multiple personal devices via Google Cloud Firestore.

1.4 Local Storage & Diagnostics

Device preferences (such as selected Qari, Arabic script type, custom calculation juristic methods, and Tasbeeh counters) are stored locally in private, sandboxed Android Room Database and DataStore tables.

Android System Permissions & Foreground Services

In strict adherence to Google Play's 2026 Foreground Service Policies, Quran Academy restricts its manifest declarations to essential runtime capabilities:

  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK: Declared specifically to sustain uninterrupted Quran audio recitations via the AndroidX Media3 ExoPlayer engine. When a user minimizes the application or locks their device, this service ensures seamless continuous recitation, maintaining an ongoing system media notification with interactive playback controls (Play, Pause, Next Ayah).
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS (Android 13+): Requested at runtime to deliver user-configured Azaan alerts at exact prayer intervals and daily morning/evening remembrance reminders. Users maintain complete granular control to toggle or mute notification categories at any time.
  • ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION & ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION: Requested with a runtime disclosure modal to derive the Qibla azimuth and astronomical twilight intervals. An IP-based offline approximation is provided as a seamless fallback if location permissions are declined.
  • RECORD_AUDIO: Activated exclusively when the user interacts with the voice search button. Used transiently for speech-to-text processing and immediately deactivated upon query completion.
  • WAKE_LOCK: Utilized during active Quran recitation and Azaan playback to prevent the Android OS from sleeping the audio decoding pipeline during screen-off intervals.

Third-Party Service Providers & Sub-Processors

To maintain high-availability infrastructure, secure authentication, and global audio content delivery, Quran Academy interfaces with vetted third-party technologies:

  • Google Mobile Ads (AdMob): Used to display family-friendly advertisements that cover server hosting, audio CDN bandwidth, and engineering maintenance costs. AdMob may process your device's Android Advertising ID (AAID) to serve contextually relevant ads and prevent click fraud.
    Opt-Out Guidance: Users can reset their Advertising ID or disable ad personalization at any time through their Android system settings: Settings > Google > Ads > Reset or Delete Advertising ID.
  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Storage): Provides enterprise-grade cloud synchronization for user bookmarks and profiles. Data stored in Google Cloud is protected by AES-256 rest encryption and TLS 1.3 transit encryption.
  • Google Gemini AI API: Powers the in-app Islamic Knowledge Assistant. Natural language queries sent to Gemini endpoints are completely decoupled from user emails and device identifiers. Inquiries are processed statelessly without retaining personal behavioral profiles.
  • EveryAyah & Islamic Network CDN: Quranic audio recitations are streamed directly from globally authenticated, open-source Islamic content delivery networks via encrypted HTTPS connections.
