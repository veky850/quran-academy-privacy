Human-Centric Privacy Commitment: Quran Academy is built with a strict "Privacy by Design" philosophy. We respect the spiritual purpose of this application. We do not harvest, monetize, or sell your personal information. This policy details our compliance with Google Play Developer Program Policies, GDPR, and CCPA.

1. Data Collection & Processing Purposes

We process minimal user data required exclusively to provide Islamic religious services:

  • Precise & Approximate Location (GPS/Network): Used locally on your device to calculate astronomical prayer times (Fajr, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib, Isha) based on your exact horizon and to determine Qibla direction. Your location coordinate data is processed in transient memory and is never stored on or transmitted to external servers.
  • Audio Data & Speech Recognition: When using the voice search feature, audio input is captured temporarily by the Android system SpeechRecognizer service to transcribe search queries for Quranic verses. Audio files are discarded immediately after transcription and are not recorded or saved.
  • Account Data (Optional): If you choose to sign in via Google Authentication, we collect your name and email address solely to sync your reading bookmarks and progress across your devices via Google Firebase. You can use the app fully offline without an account.
  • App Activity & Preferences: Reading progress, Tasbeeh counter counts, and selected Reciter settings are stored locally on your device using Android Room Database and Datastore.

2. Foreground Services & Device Hardware Usage

To deliver continuous utility without disruption, our application uses specific Android system capabilities:

  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK: Required to support background Quran audio playback. This allows you to listen to Ayah recitations seamlessly while using other applications or when the device screen is locked.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Used to deliver timely Azaan (call to prayer) alerts and daily Quranic reminders according to your custom preferences.

3. Third-Party Services & Infrastructure

We integrate with industry-standard, secure third-party SDKs that process data under strict confidentiality obligations:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore): Used for secure user authentication and cloud data synchronization.
  • Google Mobile Ads (AdMob): Displays non-intrusive advertisements to cover server bandwidth costs. AdMob may process your device Advertising ID (AAID) to deliver appropriate, family-friendly ads. You can reset or opt out of ad personalization anytime in Android System Settings > Google > Ads.
  • Google Gemini API: Powers our AI Islamic Q&A feature. Prompts sent to Gemini are anonymized and not connected to your user account or personal email.
  • EveryAyah CDN: Streams authentic Quran audio recitations directly from verified Islamic audio servers.

4. User Rights, Account & Data Deletion

We provide full control over your personal data in accordance with Google Play's User Data policy:

  • In-App Account Deletion: You can permanently delete your account and all associated cloud data at any time by navigating to Settings > Account & Data Deletion inside the app.
  • Permanent Purge: Deleting your account immediately wipes your user record from Firebase Authentication and permanently purges all your synchronized bookmarks and profile records from Google Cloud Firestore.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Quran Academy is suitable for family members of all ages. We comply with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. All content is thoroughly vetted for family-friendly environments.
